# Printer shows offline

Synthetic training article for the ServiceDesk Copilot demo. Not real vendor guidance.

## Check the connection

Confirm the network cable is seated at both ends, or that the printer is joined to the office Wi-Fi network. A printer that dropped off the network often still has power, but the computer shows it as offline.

## Power-cycle in order

Turn the printer off and wait ten seconds. Turn it on and let the startup cycle finish before restarting the print spooler on the computer. Restarting the computer first can leave the queue pointing at a stale device.

## Verify the queue

Open the printer queue and remove any document stuck in an error state. A stuck job can keep the printer marked offline even after the network link returns.
