# Print queue is stuck

Synthetic training article for the ServiceDesk Copilot demo. Not real vendor guidance.

## Pause the queue

Open the printer queue and pause it before changing anything. Pausing stops new jobs from joining the stuck document while you work.

## Clear the stuck job

Cancel the document at the top of the queue. If it does not clear, restart the print spooler service from Services, then reopen the queue and cancel the document again.

## Resume and retest

Resume the queue and print one short test page. If the same document returns to the top of the queue, the source application is resending it; close that application before clearing the queue again.
