# Toner is low

Synthetic training article for the ServiceDesk Copilot demo. Not real vendor guidance.

## Confirm the warning

Check the printer panel for the exact cartridge name and colour. A low-toner warning is advisory until the printer refuses to print, so note the message before ordering a replacement.

## Replace the cartridge

Open the front cover and wait for the cartridge holder to stop moving. Remove the old cartridge, unwrap the replacement, and rock it gently before seating it. Do not touch the imaging drum surface.

## Clear the warning

Close the cover and let the printer calibrate. If the warning remains, reseat the cartridge once; repeated warnings after a reseat are a hardware issue for the service desk.
