# Paper jam in the printer

Synthetic training article for the ServiceDesk Copilot demo. Not real vendor guidance.

## Open the correct panel

Use the panel the printer display names. Pull the tray or door slowly and support it, so the hinges are not forced. Most printers show a small diagram beside each latch.

## Remove the sheet gently

Pull the jammed sheet in the direction the paper travels. Tearing the sheet leaves fragments that cause a second jam. If the sheet will not move, stop and check for a second access panel before pulling harder.

## Check for fragments

Look inside the paper path for small pieces and remove them. Close every panel, then press the resume button so the printer re-runs its startup check.
